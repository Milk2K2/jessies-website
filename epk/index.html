Short bio

Jessie & The Revolvers are an alt-indie rock band from Stoke-on-Trent, fronted by singer-songwriter Jessie James. Driving guitars, melodic hooks and a live show built for the room. Since their 2025 debut Self Obsessed they have released three more singles, been named Best Young Act at the 2026 Music Awards of Staffordshire & Cheshire and had radio support from Radio X and BBC Introducing.
