Jessie James is a singer-songwriter from Stoke-on-Trent. She's been singing since she was about eight and taught herself guitar and piano along the way.

She writes and fronts Jessie & The Revolvers, named Best Young Act at the 2026 Music Awards of Staffordshire & Cheshire. On her own she plays solo sets around Staffordshire: her songs, and the ones a room wants to sing back.
