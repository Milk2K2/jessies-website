Who we are

It started as Jessie's idea: indie rock with a streak of country and Americana running through it. The band has dragged it somewhere louder since, with 90s guitars, a rhythm section that doesn't sit still and lyrics that don't dress anything up.

Debut single Self Obsessed came out in 2025. Long Gone, 25 and Better Off followed in 2026, along with Best Young Act at the Music Awards of Staffordshire & Cheshire and plays from Radio X and BBC Introducing.
